## High flow (both turbulent)
Scene1

**Spoken:** In a well designed geothermal system, at maximum load, the fluid flow rate is high enough for creating fully turbulent flow. Turbulence creates good mixing of the fluid which reduces the thermal resistance of the system, increasing the fluid temperature and increasing the performance of the heat pump

**On-screen text:** REYNOLDS NUMBER GREATER THAN 2,300

Turbulent flow for both probes
High heat transfer

## Reduced flow
Scene2

**Spoken:** However, during a large part of the year, the system are not running at maximum flow rates, due to lower heating requirements.

With a smooth probe, the flow becomes laminar, with poor mixing. This is bad for the heat transfer, and gives a lower fluid temperature and a lower efficiency of the heat pump.

**On-screen text:** REYNOLDS NUMBER BETWEEN 1,800 – 2,300

Laminar flow in a smooth collector

Low brine temperature and reduced COP of the heat pump
